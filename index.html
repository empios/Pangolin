pangolin / design-system / v0.1

A design system for Linux-flavored applications.

Pangolin is a synthesis. It takes the token rigor, density and component vocabulary of IBM Carbon, then drapes it in Ubuntu's warm aubergine, Ubuntu, and the humanist softness of Yaru. Built for desktop apps, web products, observability dashboards, and the occasional manifesto.

02 · BRAND IDENTITY

A mark for curl & resist.

Pangolins curl when threatened — three nested arcs into a tight spiral, perfectly tessellated. The Pangolin mark is that gesture: three concentric arcs in the Ubuntu palette, anchored by a single bright point. Together they read as motion, structure, and the iconic Ubuntu "circle of friends" — re-tuned.

03 · COLOR

Aubergine for authority. Orange for the spark.

The palette is two-and-a-half colors plus a deep neutral. Aubergine carries Ubuntu's heritage and does the heavy lifting of the brand. Orange is reserved for moments that matter: focus, selection, hover hints, charts, the occasional CTA. Warm grey replaces Carbon's cool greys throughout, so the system stays human even at 14px.

04 · TYPE

Ubuntu, set to a Carbon scale.

Type pairs Ubuntu (Dalton Maag, 2010 — humanist, distinctly Canonical) with Ubuntu Mono for code, terminals, and quantitative readouts. The scale is Carbon's productive set: 12 → 76 px, with 14 px as the workhorse body. Body lines breathe at 1.5 leading; display lines tighten to 1.0–1.2.

05 · SPACING & SHAPE

A 4 px grid with Ubuntu's softer edges.

Spacing follows Carbon's 4 px base unit, with steps at 2/4/8/12/16/24/32/40/48/64/80/96/160. Radii are Ubuntu-soft: 6 px on inputs and buttons, 8 px on cards and tiles, 12 px on dialogs and window chrome. No sharp 0 px corners unless the surface is a data table edge.

06 · MOTION

Quiet and quick.

Motion takes its cues from Carbon: short durations, productive easing. No bouncing, no overshoot. The slowest curve in the system is reserved for view transitions; everything that responds to a click sits in 70–150 ms.

07 · ICONOGRAPHY

A 20 px grid, slightly humanist.

Icons live on a 20 px grid with a 1.5 px stroke. They're drawn in the spirit of Carbon icons — geometric, even visual weight, square corners on functional glyphs — but with Yaru's pen: a touch of softness on the terminals, rounded line caps, and an occasional dot for personality.

08 · COMPONENT LIBRARY

Carbon's vocabulary, Pangolin's voice.

Twenty-two primitives, ready to compose. Heights are compact by default (32 px controls) to honour Carbon's density. Radii are Ubuntu-soft. Focus rings are always Ubuntu orange; the system reserves that color so no one mistakes a focus state for an accent.
